About Calm AI

Calm AI is an AI-powered platform designed to support people who experience panic attacks.

The platform combines Artificial Intelligence, Wearable Devices and Health Analytics.

The Problem

Millions of people suffer from panic attacks every day.

  • Fast Heart Rate
  • Difficulty Breathing
  • Dizziness
  • Fear
  • Stress
  • Unable to ask for help

Our Solution

Calm AI analyzes user health data to predict panic attacks before they happen.

  • Predict Panic Attacks
  • Breathing Exercises
  • AI Recommendations
  • Emergency Support
  • Health Dashboard
