Calm AI

From Prediction to Prevention

An AI-powered platform designed to help people manage panic attacks, understand their symptoms, and receive support at the right moment.

About Calm AI

Calm AI is a smart support system that combines artificial intelligence, data analysis, and wearable technology to help users before, during, and after panic attacks.

The project does not replace doctors or provide medical diagnosis. It works as a supportive assistant to improve the user's experience.

The Problem

Many people around the world experience sudden panic attacks that affect their daily lives.

  • Fast heartbeat
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Dizziness and losing focus
  • Feeling fear and stress
  • Difficulty asking for help during an attack

Our Solution

Calm AI provides a personalized support system that analyzes user information and helps them understand their panic patterns.

The system focuses on:

  • Monitoring health information
  • Detecting possible panic risks
  • Providing calming exercises
  • Helping users understand their triggers
