About Calm AI

Calm AI is an AI-powered platform designed to support people who experience panic attacks.

The platform combines Artificial Intelligence, Wearable Devices and Health Analytics.

The Problem

Millions of people suffer from panic attacks every day. These moments are scary and cause hard symptoms:

  • Fast Heart Rate
  • Difficulty Breathing
  • Dizziness
  • Fear and Stress
  • Unable to ask for help when it happens

Our Solution

Calm AI analyzes user health data to predict panic attacks before they happen.

  • Predict Panic Attacks early
  • Breathing Exercises to calm down
  • AI Personal Recommendations
  • Emergency Support Mode
  • Health Dashboard to see statistics

Calm AI Main Features

1. AI Panic Risk Analysis

Our app connects with smart watches to check your heart rate. If it goes too high, the app knows you feel stressed.

2. Interactive Breathing Box

A simple screen that guides you how to breathe in and out slowly when you feel panic symptoms.

3. Grounding Technique (5-4-3-2-1)

A mental game to help you focus on your room and environment instead of the fear inside your head.

4. Emergency Support Mode

One big button to send an alert message to your family or call a helper if the attack is very strong.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is a panic attack?

A1: It is a sudden feeling of intense fear or anxiety that triggers severe physical reactions when there is no real danger.

Q2: How does Calm AI help me?

A2: It monitors your heart rate and sends a warning before the attack happens, so you can start breathing exercises early.

Q3: Is my health data safe with Calm AI?

A3: Yes, your privacy is important to us. All your biological data is encrypted and secure.
